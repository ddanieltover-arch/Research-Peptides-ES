## Strategic takeaways (estimates)

1. **Spanish SERPs are under-served** relative to English competitors — prioritize ES keyword map and briefs.
2. **Trust content (COA, cold chain, compliance)** is the differentiation moat for labs and institutions.
3. Do **not** chase competitor medical claims; stay research-use-only.
4. Link opportunities: university lab blogs, Spanish biotech associations, reagent review roundups (see `link_gap_opportunities.csv`).
